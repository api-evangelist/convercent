# Convercent (by OneTrust)

> Convercent is an enterprise ethics and compliance (GRC) SaaS platform — case/helpline management, policy attestations, campaigns/disclosures, and compliance training — now part of OneTrust with customers migrating to EQS Group. It exposes compliance reporting data through an OData data-services API for BI and reporting tools.

## APIs
- [Convercent OData data services](https://support.convercent.com/hc/en-us/articles/205259429-Convercent-OData-): OData v4/v5 feeds for case management, campaigns, policy attestations, and course completions. HTTP Basic auth (Administrators/Moderators only). Base: https://api.convercent.com/OData/

## Services
- Case Management (OData v5): https://api.convercent.com/OData/case/v5/CaseManagement.svc/
- Campaign Service (OData v4): https://api.convercent.com/OData/v4/CampaignService.svc/
